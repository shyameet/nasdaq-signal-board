The setups

Crude: step 1, MCL's gate (CME micro WTI) is LONG or SHORT. Step 2, CRUDEOILM's gate (MCX mini) turns to that side after step 1 has started; if it is already on that side when step 1 starts, it doesn't count until it leaves that side and comes back. If it drops out again while the trend holds, it can sync again. Step 3, the entries, RDX WIP's four on CRUDEOILM (Rahul's RDX WIP Trend): E1, its 2m MACD (12, 26) crossing 0 that way on a closed candle, and E2, the same on its 1m MACD, while steps 1 and 2 hold, each one trade at a time (× where it ends: its MACD back through 0 against it, 16:58 New York, or its last candle of the MCX session); E3, its 9m and 11m waves that way, its 5m EMA 21 on that side of the 47 and its 5m EMA 8 crossing back over the 21, and E4, the same on its 3m, while step 1 holds (as on Rahul's chart, they don't wait for step 2). Times shown are the candle's close, IST.

Nat Gas: the same, with NG (CME natural gas) in step 1 and NATGASMINI (MCX) in steps 2 and 3.

Gold, Silver, Copper: the same again, with MGC, SIL and MHG (CME micros) in step 1 and GOLDM (MCX gold mini), SILVERM (MCX silver mini) and COPPER (MCX) in steps 2 and 3. A setup shows greyed out until the VPS records both of its markets.

The gate

For each market on its own candles, RDX WIP's trend (Rahul's RDX WIP Trend): LONG when the 5m, 9m and 11m waves are all up (on each of them, EMA 8 − EMA 47 and EMA 21 − EMA 47 both above 0) and the 5m EMA 21 is above the EMA 47; SHORT when all of that is the other way; NO TRADE otherwise. It is read on candle close: a 5m, 9m or 11m candle counts from the minute it closes. Exactly the Nasdaq pages' gate. Each indicator is warmed up on the saved days before, so it reads what it would have read live.

Trends

A trend is one unbroken run of step 1 in one direction: any NO TRADE minute ends it. Every entry in a trend is shown.

Charts

The CME future (MCL or NG) on the left and the MCX mini (CRUDEOILM or NATGASMINI) on the right, side by side and scrolling together (both end at the same time and show as many candles each, so the CME's longer hours just scroll past), each with its Entry pane (the 1m, 2m and 3m MACD) and Trend pane (the 5m, 9m and 11m waves), and the 5m EMA 8, 21 and 47 on the price chart, colored by their stack (green or red stacked, yellow a shallow pullback, orange a deeper one). Drag the thin line between the two charts, or between a price chart and its MACD panes, to size them as you like (remembered; double-click a line for the default). Each chart and its panes are green where every Trend wave switched on is above 0 (with all six on, that market's own RDX WIP trend), red where every one is below (the gate is shown in words, in the step chips); the MCX mini shows its entries (arrows). The chips in the CME chart's header show steps 1 and 3, the trend and the entry (point at one for the details). Candles start at each market's open, as on TradingView: the CME's at 17:00 Chicago (03:30 IST, 04:30 IST in Chicago's winter time), MCX's at 09:00 IST. Times are IST.

Days

A day starts at the CME open, 17:00 Chicago: 03:30 IST (04:30 IST from 2 Nov, when Chicago leaves summer time). So Monday's day begins at 03:30 IST on Monday with the Globex session that opened on Sunday evening in Chicago, and holds that whole CME session and MCX's day. The picked day opens on its own, with the two days before it to its left; scroll to the left edge and older days load in. The calendar sets the newest day shown. The step chips count everything loaded.

Data

Saved 1-minute candles from our VPS, from 15 Oct 2026, one file per UTC day: MCL and NG from NinjaTrader (the front month, moving to the next on NinjaTrader's rollover date), CRUDEOILM and NATGASMINI from Dhan (the near month, moving to the next on its expiry day). At a switch the older candles are moved by the gap between the two contracts, so the chart and its indicators run on smoothly and the newest candles are the contract traded now, at its own prices; a yellow mark shows where it switched, and a yellow note warns on the switch day and the trading day before it (the chart is only a chart: check the contract and its expiry on the platform you trade on). Nothing is filled in: a minute with no trade has no candle. A trading day is listed once its own UTC file is saved (the file before it holds only its first hours); the page reads the saved files only, so reload it for what the VPS has saved since.
