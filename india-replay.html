The setups

Nifty: step 1, NIFTY 50 and GIFT NIFTY show the same gate (both LONG or both SHORT). Step 2, RELIANCE's gate turns to that side after step 1 has started; if RELIANCE is already on that side when step 1 starts, it doesn't count until it leaves that side and comes back (it has to be the odd man out). If it drops out again while the trend holds, it can sync again. Step 3, the entry: RELIANCE's 2m or 3m MACD crosses 0 that way on a closed candle while steps 1 and 2 hold (times shown are the candle's close).

Bank Nifty: the same, with NIFTY BANK alone in step 1 and HDFC BANK in steps 2 and 3.

The gate

For each market on its own candles: LONG when the 5m, 9m and 11m MACD are all above 0 and the 3m EMA 21 is above the EMA 47; SHORT when all of that is the other way; NO TRADE otherwise. Exactly the Nasdaq pages' gate. Each indicator is warmed up on the saved days before, so it reads what it would have read live.

Trends

A trend is one unbroken run of step 1 in one direction: any NO TRADE minute ends it. Every entry in a trend is shown.

Charts

Nifty: one chart is big, with its Entry (2m/3m) and Trend MACD panes; the others are small tiles on the right, showing the whole day with their gate. Click a tile, use the switch in the big chart's header, or press 1, 2 or 3 to make another chart big. Bank Nifty: the two charts side by side, scrolling together. Drag the thin lines between the charts, between the tiles, or between a price chart and its MACD panes to size them as you like (remembered; double-click a line for the default). The index is shaded by step 1; GIFT NIFTY by its own gate (its whole session, 06:30 to 02:45 the next morning); the stock where step 2 holds, with its sync points (dots) and entries (arrows). The chips in the big chart's header show steps 1, 2 and 3 (point at one for the details). Candles start at each market's session open (09:15 for NSE, 06:30 for GIFT NIFTY), as on TradingView. Times are IST.

Days

The picked day opens on its own, with the two days before it to its left; scroll to the left edge and older days load in. The calendar sets the newest day shown. The step chips count everything loaded.

Data

Dhan's 1-minute candles, saved by our VPS after each close, from 15 Oct 2026. Nothing is filled in: a minute with no trade has no candle. NSE stocks stop at 15:14 (the closing auction follows); the indices stay flat from 15:15 to 15:27, then move to the closing price at 15:28, as Dhan reports them.
