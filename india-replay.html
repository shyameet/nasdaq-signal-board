The setups

Nifty: step 1, NIFTY 50 and GIFT NIFTY show the same gate (both LONG or both SHORT). Step 2, RELIANCE's gate turns to that side after step 1 has started; if RELIANCE is already on that side when step 1 starts, it doesn't count until it leaves that side and comes back (it has to be the odd man out). If it drops out again while the trend holds, it can sync again. Step 3, the entries, RDX WIP's four on RELIANCE (Rahul's RDX WIP Trend): E1, its 2m MACD (12, 26) crossing 0 that way on a closed candle, and E2, the same on its 1m MACD, while steps 1 and 2 hold, each one trade at a time (× where it ends: its MACD back through 0 against it, 16:58 New York, or its last candle of the day (15:14, where Dhan's stock candles stop)); E3, its 9m and 11m waves that way, its 5m EMA 21 on that side of the 47 and its 5m EMA 8 crossing back over the 21, and E4, the same on its 3m, while step 1 holds (as on Rahul's chart, they don't wait for step 2). Times shown are the candle's close.

Bank Nifty: the same, with NIFTY BANK alone in step 1 and HDFC BANK in steps 2 and 3.

The stock in steps 2 and 3 is picked in the list beside the setup, among the index's five biggest by weight (NSE, 30 Sep 2026): Nifty HDFC BANK, ICICI BANK, RELIANCE, BHARTI AIRTEL, L&T; Bank Nifty HDFC BANK, ICICI BANK, KOTAK BANK, AXIS BANK, SBI. Step 1 stays the same. A stock shows "no data yet" until the VPS records it.

The gate

For each market on its own candles, RDX WIP's trend (Rahul's RDX WIP Trend): LONG when the 5m, 9m and 11m waves are all up (on each of them, EMA 8 − EMA 47 and EMA 21 − EMA 47 both above 0) and the 5m EMA 21 is above the EMA 47; SHORT when all of that is the other way; NO TRADE otherwise. It is read on candle close: a 5m, 9m or 11m candle counts from the minute it closes. Exactly the Nasdaq pages' gate. Each indicator is warmed up on the saved days before, so it reads what it would have read live.

Trends

A trend is one unbroken run of step 1 in one direction: any NO TRADE minute ends it. Every entry in a trend is shown.

Charts

The charts side by side, equal widths: Nifty's three (NIFTY 50, GIFT NIFTY, the stock), Bank Nifty's two; each with its Entry pane (the 1m, 2m and 3m MACD) and Trend pane (the 5m, 9m and 11m waves), and the 5m EMA 8, 21 and 47 on the price chart, colored by their stack (green or red stacked, yellow a shallow pullback, orange a deeper one). All the charts end at the same time and show as many candles each: drag or zoom any of them and the others follow (GIFT NIFTY's night hours then simply scroll past). Drag the thin lines between the charts, or between a price chart and its MACD panes, to size them as you like (remembered; double-click a line for the default). Each chart and its panes are green where every Trend wave switched on is above 0 (with all six on, that market's own RDX WIP trend), red where every one is below (the gate is shown in words in the step chips); GIFT NIFTY shows its whole session, 06:30 to 02:45 the next morning; the stock shows its entries (arrows). The chips in the index chart's header show steps 1 and 3, the trend and the entry (point at one for the details). Candles start at each market's session open (09:15 for NSE, 06:30 for GIFT NIFTY), as on TradingView. Times are IST.

Days

The picked day opens on its own, with the two days before it to its left; scroll to the left edge and older days load in. The calendar sets the newest day shown. The step chips count everything loaded.

Data

Dhan's 1-minute candles, saved by our VPS after each close, from 15 Oct 2026. Nothing is filled in: a minute with no trade has no candle. NSE stocks stop at 15:14 (the closing auction follows); the indices stay flat from 15:15 to 15:27, then move to the closing price at 15:28, as Dhan reports them.
