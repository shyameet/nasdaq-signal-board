How this test works, and what it does not show
  • Settings never re-fitted. The pass sizing and the funded setup were picked on 10 Aug - 13 Sep only. The Scalper's own settings (17.5 pt target, 65 pt stop) were set in mid-October on 5-16 Oct, so "Last 2 weeks" (from 26 Oct) is unseen for both; "1 Oct - 9 Nov" includes days the Scalper's settings were set on.
  • Pass mode, every evaluation day: day stop = the smaller of a share of the daily loss limit and the room above the max-loss floor divided by k; size = s × (day stop ÷ (65 pt stop + 1.5 pt spread)), cut to the firm's leverage; day target = what the phase still needs, spread over the planned days. The bot closes the day at the target or the stop (open trades included), else at the Scalper's own exits.
  • Funded: a fixed size with a fixed day target / day stop, picked for payouts; payouts on each firm's schedule and split.
  • Prices: MNQ futures 1-minute prices stand in for the firms' Nasdaq CFD (spread 1.5 pts a trade, the futures fee dropped, a tick of slippage on stops). The firms' own MT5 feeds are not connected yet; their quotes, spreads and session hours differ a little.
  • Same-day accounts: evaluations bought on the same session trade identically.
  • Not modelled: news windows, swaps (flat before the reset), FTMO's 19:00 ET reset in the 26-30 Oct clock-change week, the firms' bot rules (FundedNext: paid EA add-on, under $50K).

How this works, and what it does not show
  • Feeds: 1-minute bid bars and the spread of each minute from the FTMO (US100.cash, XAUUSD) and FundedNext (NDX100, XAUUSD) free-trial MT5 terminals on the VPS, 30 Jul onward. Both servers run on New York time + 7 h. Price used = bid + half the spread; the spread of the entry minute is paid at entry; a tick of slippage on every exit.
  • Futures signals: the Scalper decides on MNQ futures bars exactly as our bots do, and each entry, target and stop is then filled on the firm's own prices. Own signals: the Scalper's signals worked out on the firm's own bars, as an MT5 bot would see them.
  • Hours: FTMO's Nasdaq and gold stop 16:50 - 18:05 ET (no entry inside; an open trade closes at 16:49); FundedNext's run to 17:00 and reopen at 18:00 like the futures. Gold enters at the close of the firm's first minute (FTMO 18:06, FundedNext 18:01) instead of the futures' 18:00.
  • Modes: Fast pass = day stop 90% of the daily limit, size twice what a full stop fits in it, target spread over the minimum days + 2. Steady pass = the same day stop, size so one full stop always fits, target spread over 10 days. Forward-test pick = the frozen pass setup of the Forex paper test. The funded stage always uses the frozen funded setup (Scalper) or a full gold stop = half the daily limit (gold).
  • Buying: 1 at a time = the next evaluation the session after one passes or fails. Every session / Mon-Wed-Fri = one new evaluation each such session while fewer than 10 are open. Funded accounts are not capped (FTMO's $400K limit is not modelled).
  • Periods: "19 Oct - now" is unseen by every setting (the Scalper's own settings were set on 5-16 Oct). "10 Aug - 16 Oct" includes the days the settings were picked on, so it flatters them; those accounts stop on 16 Oct.
  • Not modelled: news windows (FTMO funded: no trade 2 min around high-impact news), swaps (no Scalper or gold trade is held past 17:00 ET), FTMO's 19:00 reset in the 26-30 Oct clock-change week, the firms' bot rules (FundedNext: paid EA add-on, accounts under $50K). FundingPips, The5ers and Tradeify have no feed here.

How we buy

Trend (August settings) at 50K: 2 NQ to pass, then 3 micros funded; a funded day opens no new trade once its closed trades reach +$250 or −$600.

WF4 and Scalper 3m: "Once a minute" checks the day target and stop on each minute bar, "Every tick" on every MNQ tick. Both book the target as an exact resting limit (filled once the price trades a tick through it, at the limit's price) and the stop as a resting stop (its level after the measured slippage, or worse when the price jumps past it).

Our daily loss limit
  • What it does: for gold, each night's trade risks at most 80% of the smaller of the room left and this limit (Gold ONS trades a fixed size; its day is cut at the limit). It never fails the account by itself.
  • What it does: once an account is down half its max loss in a day ($1,000 at 50K, $500 at 25K; $750 at FundedNext Flex 50K), the bot stops for the day. It never fails the account by itself.
