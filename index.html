How it works

The signal

Both panels apply the same four rules, each on its own candle size, checked every second on the bar still forming:

RuleBullishBearish
3mEMA 8 above EMA 21EMA 8 below EMA 21
5mMACD above 0MACD below 0
7mMACD above 0MACD below 0
15mMACD above its signalMACD below its signal

All four bullish: BULLISH. All four bearish: BEARISH. Anything else: NEUTRAL. MACD is 12/26/9.

The two panels

NDX10 combines the 10 largest Nasdaq-100 weights (official index weights rescaled to 100%, Hyperliquid prices; 1000 = the basket's reference close). NAS100 runs the same rules on the Nasdaq-100 perp itself, XYZ100; its 1m MACD (the first pane under its price chart) is for information only. The panes run from the shortest candle to the longest.

The charts

Green zones on the price charts are where a panel's signal was bullish at the candle's close, red zones where it was bearish; the forming candle shows it live. ▲ BUY marks the candle where the signal turns bullish, ▼ SELL where it turns bearish. The amber and blue lines are the 3m EMA 8 and EMA 21 that the 3m rule tests, as of each candle's close. On the left chart they are the basket's EMAs scaled to XYZ100's price: EMA 21 sits on XYZ100's EMA 21 and EMA 8 keeps the basket's own gap to it, so they cross exactly when the left 3m rule flips. Times are in your timezone.

Drawing tools

The toolbar on the left draws on the price charts: trend line, horizontal and vertical line, rectangle, Fib retracement and measure. Drag from one point to the other, or click one and then the other. Click a drawing to select it, drag it or its handles to move it, and press Delete to remove it. Esc cancels, Ctrl+Z undoes, and Shift+drag measures without picking the tool. Drawings show on both price charts and are saved in this browser.

Data

Prices stream live from Hyperliquid's public API, around the clock, and the signals are recalculated every second. The page keeps nothing about you; your drawings and settings stay in this browser. For information only, not investment advice.
