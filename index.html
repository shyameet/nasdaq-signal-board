    How it works

    The gate

    The gate runs on your stocks' composite, checked every second on the bars still forming:

    GateCondition
    LONG5m, 9m and 11m MACD all above 0, and the 3m EMA 21 above the EMA 47
    SHORTall three below 0, and the EMA 21 below the EMA 47
    NO TRADEanything else

    It shades the chart and the MACD panes, and sets the alerts. The header shows the gate and its four conditions. MACD is 12/26/9.

    Your stocks

    The chart is your own composite of the stocks you pick (Stocks button, top right; the 10 largest Nasdaq-100 stocks that trade on Hyperliquid by default, plus AVGO, TSM and INTC; any 1 to 13 of them). Each stock you pick counts equally, so the composite is 1000 at the basket's reference close and each minute's close is 1000 times the average of the stocks' moves since then. The gate is LONG only when every stock you pick is LONG on its own candles, SHORT only when every one is SHORT, and NO TRADE otherwise; the rule tiles show the composite's own values. Each minute's open is the previous minute's close and its high and low the larger and smaller of the two (live candles also catch the highs and lows in between), so older candles have shorter wicks than live ones. Volume is the stocks' combined dollar volume. The pick is shared with the other pages and kept in this browser.

    Under the price chart are two MACD panes (12/26/9). Entry: 2m and 3m, for timing; they are not part of the gate. Trend: 5m, 9m and 11m, the gate's three MACD rules, plus 7m and 15m; each with its signal line, dashed in the same color (the "signals" switch hides them all). Each wave has two colors of its own, one while its MACD is above 0 and another while below, so its color changes where it crosses zero; the chips in a pane's label show each wave's pair (left: above 0, right: below). Each line shows its value as of every candle's close, so a longer MACD steps along on shorter candles. Click a name in a pane's label to switch that line off or on; the choice is remembered in this browser, on both pages. The price chart and both panes are coloured by the Trend waves you switch on: green where every one of them is above 0, red where every one is below (no colour with all of them off); the gate itself is the word in the header. Dashed vertical lines mark where a new day begins in your timezone.

    The charts

    Zones: green while the gate is LONG (labeled ▲ LONG), red while it is SHORT, no color for NO TRADE. The zone is the gate, not the candle: a red candle can sit in a green zone. The forming candle shows the gate live. The amber and blue lines are your stocks' 3m EMA 21 and EMA 47 that the gate tests, as of each candle's close. Times are in your timezone.

    Drawing tools

    The sidebar on the left draws on the price chart: vertical line, horizontal line and measure (drag from one point to the other, or click one and then the other). Click a line to select it, drag it to move it, and press Delete to remove it. Esc cancels, Ctrl+Z undoes, and Shift+drag measures without picking the tool. The arrow at the bottom of the sidebar hides it. Drawings are saved in this browser.
