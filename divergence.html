    How it works

    What this page shows

    Each series has a gate: LONG when its 5m, 9m and 11m MACD are all above 0 and its 3m EMA 21 is above the EMA 47, SHORT when all of that is the other way, NO TRADE otherwise. This page compares the gate of the stocks (the 10 biggest Nasdaq-100 stocks combined, or your own pick of them: their official weights are rescaled to 100%, rebuilt with no new download) with the gate of the Nasdaq-100 itself (XYZ100), and marks only where they differ; when they agree, the chart stays clean.

    The marks

    Index alone: the index's gate is LONG while the stocks' isn't. Stocks first: the stocks' gate is LONG while the index's isn't yet. Other: they differ in another way. These have not been studied for the gate yet, so the page makes no claim about what follows them.

    The MACD pane

    Under the chart are XYZ100's two MACD panes (12/26/9). Entry: 2m and 3m, for timing; they are not part of the gate. Trend: 5m, 9m and 11m, the gate's three MACD rules, plus 1m, 7m and 15m; each with its signal line, dashed in the same color (the "signals" switch hides them all). Each line shows its value as of every candle's close, so a longer MACD steps along on shorter candles. Click a name in a pane's label to switch that line off or on; the choice is remembered in this browser, on both pages. Both panes are shaded by the combined gate: green where both gates are LONG, red where both are SHORT. Dashed vertical lines mark where a new day begins in your timezone.
