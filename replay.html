    How it works

    What you see

    One UTC day at a time (00:00 to 24:00 UTC; times are shown in your timezone). Left: your stocks' composite (Stocks button; the same pick as the other pages, applied to every past day). Right: the Nasdaq-100 perp, XYZ100. Both get exactly the same treatment: candles, the gate's zones, the 3m EMA 21 and EMA 47, the Entry (2m/3m) and Trend MACD panes. Each indicator is warmed up on the day before, so it reads what it would have read live.

    The gate

    LONG when the 5m, 9m and 11m MACD are all above 0 and the 3m EMA 21 is above the EMA 47; SHORT when all of that is the other way; NO TRADE otherwise. Each chart uses its own gate; its header also shows the other chart's gate at the same moment.

    Signals

    A 2m (or 3m) signal is that scalper's MACD line crossing 0 on a closed candle the same way the chart's gate points at that candle's close: crossing up while the gate is LONG, down while it is SHORT. Crosses against the gate are ignored. Per trend keeps only the first 1 or 2 signals of each gate trend (or all). A trend is one run of the gate in one direction; Gap lets the gate sit at NO TRADE for that many minutes without ending the trend (Off: any NO TRADE ends it). One trend per day keeps only signals in the direction of the day's first gate trend. Only signals are shown: no exits or results.

    Playback

    Whole day shows the full day. Switch it off to replay: ⏭ and ⏮ step one candle, Play runs at the chosen speed, and only what was known at the replay time is drawn (closed MACD bars, the gate up to that minute, signals so far). The signal buttons jump between signals.

    Data

    Days come from our own daily archive of Hyperliquid's 1-minute candles (from 18 Oct 2026; that day only from its evening) and, for the last few days, straight from Hyperliquid, which keeps about 3.5 days of 1-minute candles. Nothing is filled in or made up: a minute with no trade is a flat bar at the last price, as on the exchange.
