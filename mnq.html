What you see

Two copies side by side. Each: the real MNQ future (CME micro Nasdaq-100, NinjaTrader on our VPS) with its gate's shading (green LONG, red SHORT) and the 5m EMA 8, 21 and 47 (colored by their stack: green or red stacked, yellow a shallow pullback, orange a deeper one), and under it three panes with the same lines: the 1m, 2m and 3m MACD (12, 26; shown only, on a scale of their own in each pane so the far bigger waves don't flatten them, 0 shared) and the 5m, 9m, 11m and 15m waves (EMA 8 − EMA 47 and EMA 21 − EMA 47 on each). The right copy's panes also have the 1h waves (the same two on the 1-hour candles, from our nightly files and today's minutes). MNQ's bars start at its session open (17:00 Chicago), as on TradingView. Both copies scroll and zoom together.

Basket 1 and Basket 2: composites of your own stocks from Hyperliquid, each picked with the button on its row; each copy has its own two (the left's Basket 1 is the pick the other pages use); each stock counts equally. They are drawn on MNQ's candles: every point is the basket's line as of that MNQ candle's close. MNQ: MNQ's own. Each pane is shaded by its own trend.

An arrow on an MNQ chart marks where an MNQ alone stretch (MNQ's gate is LONG, that copy's Basket 1's isn't) or a Stocks first stretch (Basket 1's gate is LONG, MNQ's not yet) begins. The badge in the top bar says where the left copy stands right now (on a past day, at MNQ's last candle of it).

The gate

From the lines switched on (click a line's name in any pane to switch it; the switch holds on every pane and every tab): LONG when every one of them is above 0, SHORT when every one is below, NO TRADE otherwise. On the left the 5m, 9m, 11m and 15m waves count; on the right the 1h waves too. The 1m, 2m and 3m MACD are shown only. With the 5m, 9m and 11m lines on and the 15m and 1h ones off it is RDX WIP's trend (Rahul's RDX WIP Trend). It is read on candle close: a candle counts from the minute it closes (an hour's from the minute that ends it). MNQ and each basket have their own gate (a basket's: every picked stock's own lines must agree; its pane is shaded by the basket's own lines); each header shows all three at the same moment.

Loading: a page opens at once on the saved days and on the minutes this browser saved; the newest minutes come in from Hyperliquid a moment later. A note at the bottom says when Hyperliquid is busy or not answering, and they come in once it does.

Days

A day is a CME trading day: it starts at the CME open, 17:00 Chicago (03:30 IST, or 04:30 IST from 2 Nov when Chicago leaves summer time). Saturday and Sunday trading days have no CME session: the header says CME closed – weekend, and as every pane follows MNQ's candles, nothing new shows until the CME opens (the stocks still trade). The picked day opens on its own, with the two days before it to its left; scroll to the left edge and older days load in. The calendar sets the newest day shown. The marks cover everything loaded.

Data

Stocks: our daily archive of Hyperliquid's 1-minute candles and, for the last days, Hyperliquid itself; today updates every minute. MNQ: the VPS's saved 1-minute candles from 15 Oct 2026 (NinjaTrader's front month, moving to the next on its rollover date: the older candles are then moved by the gap between the two contracts so the chart runs on smoothly, a yellow mark shows the switch, and a yellow note warns on the switch day and the trading day before it; check the contract and its expiry on the platform you trade on). Each UTC day is saved shortly after it ends (00:45 UTC, 06:15 IST), so the newest MNQ candles are from the day before. Nothing is filled in: a minute with no trade has no candle.
