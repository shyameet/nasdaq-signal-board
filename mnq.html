    What you see

    Left: your stocks' composite from Hyperliquid (Stocks button; the same pick as the other pages, each stock counting equally, its gate LONG or SHORT only when every picked stock agrees). Right: the real MNQ future (CME micro Nasdaq-100, NinjaTrader on our VPS). Both get the same gate, the 5m EMA 8, 21 and 47 (colored by their stack: green or red stacked, yellow a shallow pullback, orange a deeper one), the Entry pane (the 1m, 2m and 3m MACD) and the Trend pane (the 5m, 9m and 11m waves). The price charts are tinted where the two gates disagree: MNQ alone (MNQ's gate is LONG, your stocks' isn't), Stocks first (your stocks' gate is LONG, MNQ's not yet), and grey for any other difference; an arrow marks where an MNQ-alone or stocks-first stretch begins. The badge in the top bar says where they stand right now (on a past day, at MNQ's last candle of it). MNQ's bars start at its session open (17:00 Chicago), as on TradingView.

    The gate

    RDX WIP's trend (Rahul's RDX WIP Trend): LONG when the 5m, 9m and 11m waves are all up (on each of them, EMA 8 − EMA 47 and EMA 21 − EMA 47 both above 0) and the 5m EMA 21 is above the EMA 47; SHORT when all of that is the other way; NO TRADE otherwise. It is read on candle close: a 5m, 9m or 11m candle counts from the minute it closes. Each chart uses its own gate; its header also shows the other chart's gate at the same moment.

    Days

    A day is a CME trading day: it starts at the CME open, 17:00 Chicago (03:30 IST, or 04:30 IST from 2 Nov when Chicago leaves summer time), on both charts. Saturday and Sunday trading days have no CME session: the MNQ chart says CME closed – weekend (the stocks still trade). The picked day opens on its own, with the two days before it to its left; scroll to the left edge and older days load in. The calendar sets the newest day shown. The marks cover everything loaded.

    Data

    Stocks: our daily archive of Hyperliquid's 1-minute candles and, for the last days, Hyperliquid itself; today updates every minute. MNQ: the VPS's saved 1-minute candles from 15 Oct 2026 (NinjaTrader's front month, moving to the next on its rollover date: the older candles are then moved by the gap between the two contracts so the chart runs on smoothly, a yellow mark shows the switch, and a yellow note warns on the switch day and the trading day before it; check the contract and its expiry on the platform you trade on). Each UTC day is saved shortly after it ends (00:45 UTC, 06:15 IST), so the newest MNQ candles are from the day before. Nothing is filled in: a minute with no trade has no candle.
